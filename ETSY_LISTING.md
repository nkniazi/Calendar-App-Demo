## Description

**The planner that tells you if your life plan is realistic.**

LifeBalance Planner is a digital planner that runs in your web browser — no app to install, no account to create, no subscription. Just open the HTML file and start planning.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need internet to use this?**
A: No. After downloading, everything runs offline in your browser.

**Q: Will my data be safe?**
A: Your data stays in your browser's local storage on your device. We recommend exporting a backup weekly from Settings.

**Q: Can I use this on my iPad?**
A: The included GoodNotes Companion PDF is designed for iPad with hyperlinked tabs for easy navigation. The HTML planner is designed for desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge) on Windows and Mac.

**Q: Does this work with GoodNotes/Notability?**
A: The companion PDF planner works great in GoodNotes and Notability. The main HTML planner runs in a web browser.

**Q: What does "AI-Ready" mean?**
A: The planner has buttons that copy your schedule data as prompts you can paste into ChatGPT or Claude. No AI runs inside the planner itself — your data never leaves your device.

**Q: Can I use this for multiple years?**
A: Yes! The planner is completely undated and works for any year.

**Q: What if I switch computers?**
A: Export your backup on the old computer, copy files to the new one, open the HTML file, and import your backup.
