# LifeBalance AI — Monetization Strategy

**Status:** Recommendation only. No payments implemented.

---

## Recommended Model: Freemium with AI Usage Limit

### Free Tier

Everything that works without AI API calls:

- Full calendar (all 6 views)
- Unlimited events, tasks, goals
- Up to 5 habits
- 7 default life categories + 3 custom
- Life Balance Score
- 24-hour budget tracking
- Recurring events (all patterns)
- Data export/import (JSON + ICS)
- Dark mode
- **3 AI requests per day** (plan day, plan week, what now, commands)
- Demo/sample data

### Paid Tier ($5-8/month or $48-72/year)

- **Unlimited AI planning** — no daily request limit
- **Unlimited habits** (free tier: 5)
- **Unlimited custom categories** (free tier: 3 custom beyond defaults)
- **Weekly AI insights** — automatic analysis of schedule patterns
- **Trend analytics** — how balance score changes over weeks/months
- **Priority support**

### Why This Split Works

1. **Free tier is genuinely useful.** Calendar, tasks, habits, goals, and budget work fully. Users can experience the product's core value without paying.

2. **AI is the upgrade trigger.** After 3 daily AI requests, users who find it valuable will hit the limit. The friction is at the moment of highest perceived value.

3. **Habits and categories scale.** Power users who track 10+ habits or create many custom categories are the most engaged — and most likely to pay.

4. **No feature gating on core calendar.** Users never feel locked out of basic functionality. The upgrade is about depth and intelligence, not access.

---

## What Is Valuable Enough to Pay For

Based on the actual product:

1. **"What Now?" with full context** — This is the killer feature. A user asking "what should I do?" 5+ times a day will quickly hit the free limit and value the upgrade.

2. **Weekly AI planning** — Users who plan their entire week with AI save 30+ minutes of manual planning. The time savings alone justify $5-8/month.

3. **Habit tracking at scale** — 5 habits covers basics; serious self-improvement users want 10-20.

4. **Trend analysis (future)** — "Your work-life balance improved 15% this month" is motivating and sticky.

---

## What NOT to Gate

- Calendar views
- Event creation/editing
- Basic goals and budget
- Data export
- Dark mode
- Recurring events

These are table stakes. Gating them would feel punitive and drive users to free alternatives.

---

## Revenue Projections (Illustrative)

At $6/month, $60/year:
- 1,000 free users → ~5% conversion → 50 paid users → $3,000/year
- 10,000 free users → ~5% conversion → 500 paid users → $30,000/year
- 100,000 free users → ~3% conversion → 3,000 paid users → $180,000/year

These are conservative SaaS conversion benchmarks. AI-first products with clear value often see 8-12% conversion.
